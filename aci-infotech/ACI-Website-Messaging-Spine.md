# ACI Website Messaging Spine

Working reference. The lines every page reuses, so the whole site sounds like one voice and tells one story. Last updated 22 June 2026.

Spine decisions: the core is Data and AI together, not AI alone. Outcome and industry led. The rest of the services are the foundation underneath. Proof is the glue. Voice is simple and direct. No em dashes. Nothing salesy. Every claim earns its place with a number or a named system.

## The one idea

ACI delivers data and AI outcomes for enterprises in their own industry, engineered, shipped, and run in production.

Data and AI lead together, on purpose. Data is half of what ACI sells and the reason the AI works. Lead with AI alone and we undersell the data business and sound like every other AI shop.

The wedge: most enterprise AI fails because the data underneath it is not ready. ACI builds both, so it ships and runs. Few competitors can honestly say they do the data foundation and the AI on top, at production scale, and prove it. That pairing is the whole position. Say it as cause and effect, not as two items on a menu.

## Hero options

1. Headline: "Data and AI that ships." Subhead: "Most AI fails on bad data. We build both, in production. 280+ systems, $1B+ delivered."
2. Headline: "From data foundation to AI outcomes." Subhead: "Engineered, shipped, and run by industry. 280+ systems in production."
3. Headline: "Your data, AI-ready. Your AI, in production." Subhead: "We do both, end to end, for financial services, retail, manufacturing, and more."

Keep "Enterprise technology. Delivered." available as the short brand line. "Data + AI" works as a visual shorthand in headers and graphics.

## Value pillars

Four themes. Every service page and industry page should ladder up to one of these.

1. Data and AI, together. The AI works only when the data under it is AI-ready, and we are the ones who engineer it to be. That pairing is the differentiator, not two separate menus.
2. We start with your outcome, by industry. We map the operation first, then build for the result you need, not a generic platform.
3. We ship to production and run it. Past the pilot, into the live environment, with the same security and change discipline as anything else you run.
4. We have the receipts. 280+ systems in production, $1B+ delivered, 95% retention, 20 years.

## ArqAI Labs on the ACI site

Light touch, partner framed. ArqAI Labs is an independent AI company and ACI's strategic AI partner, not ACI's platform, division, or arm. (The correct name is "ArqAI Labs", not "ArqAI".)

- Approved: "accelerated by ArqAI Labs, our strategic AI partner," with ACI's own proof leading. "ArqAI Labs is an independent AI company that designs, builds, and runs production AI agents in regulated operations, with governance on every action."
- Do not say: "our proprietary platform," "ACI's AI platform / vertical / division," "ArqAI ships with every engagement," "nothing to license, nothing to deploy," or "wing / arm / owned by" for either company.
- ACI's own data and AI proof carries the story. ArqAI Labs is a mention and proof of depth, never the engine.
- The clean line between the two: ACI sells AI outcomes by industry, on a foundation it builds and runs. ArqAI Labs is production operational AI, the vertical agents with governance on every action. Keep them in their lanes.

## Voice rules

- Short sentences. Plain words. Say the thing.
- Proof over adjectives. A number beats "leading" or "best-in-class."
- "We build it, we ship it, we run it" is the register. Not consulting-speak.
- No em dashes anywhere. No "leverage," "synergy," "cutting-edge," "world-class," "transform your business."
- Active voice. ACI does things. "We built," not "solutions were delivered."

## Words to use and avoid

Use: build, ship, run, in production, outcome, foundation, by industry, the receipts, named systems and numbers.

Avoid: proprietary platform (for ArqAI), leverage, synergy, holistic, best-in-class, world-class, next-generation, seamless, empower, unlock, journey, em dashes.
